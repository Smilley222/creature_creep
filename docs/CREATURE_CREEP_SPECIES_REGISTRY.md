# CREATURE CREEP — SPECIES REGISTRY

**Project:** Creature Creep V6  
**Document:** Standalone creature design reference  
**Status:** Design draft — not implemented in the playable game  
**Scope:** 12 base species; 4 established and 8 proposed

## Purpose
This is the single design reference for creatures. Keep species definitions separate from storyline, life paths, and executable game code. The Keeper, Outlaw, and Masked Pup paths share the same creature species and core battle rules.

## Core rules
- **Elements:** Ignis 🔥, Flora 🌿, Volt ⚡, Umbra 🌑.
- **Advantage cycle:** Ignis > Flora > Volt > Umbra > Ignis.
- **Battles:** 1v1, four equipped moves, party of up to six.
- **Out-of-battle:** One selected lead creature follows the player and can use a field skill.
- **Capture:** Creature HP, status, species difficulty and Creep Core quality affect success.
- **Bond:** 0–100, grows through shared activity; bond can unlock interactions but never makes creatures randomly ignore battle commands.
- **Evolution:** Can be postponed. Some future species will have branching evolutions.
- **Design distinction:** Masked Pup refers to the human player's chosen identity; creatures are nonsexual companions and battle partners.

## Species profiles

### CC-001 — Cindra 🔥
- **Concept:** Small fire fox with an ember-bright tail.
- **Battle identity:** Fragile but quick, excels at applying burn and finishing weakened foes.
- **Suggested starting moves:** Ember Bite, Flame Rush, Heat Haze, Inferno Fang (later unlock).
- **Field skill — Emberlight:** Lights unlit torches and clears designated dry brambles.
- **Habitat:** Havenwood clearings and warm edges of Ashfall Ridge.
- **Evolution:** Cindra → Pyroclast (Lv 16) → Solvyr (Lv 36).
- **Collection hook:** Fast offensive starter for players who like aggressive battles.
- **Status:** Established species and evolution line; move availability still needs balancing.

### CC-002 — Thornback 🌿
- **Concept:** Moss-covered tortoise with a leafy shell.
- **Battle identity:** Durable protector with healing and control moves.
- **Suggested starting moves:** Vine Strike, Shell Guard, Nature Mend, Root Snare.
- **Field skill — Rootcall:** Activates designated root bridges and plant barriers.
- **Habitat:** Havenwood woodland and shaded springs.
- **Evolution:** Thornback → Mosswarden (Lv 18) → Terragrove (Lv 38).
- **Collection hook:** First Rowan quest creature; can open a hidden Havenwood passage.
- **Status:** Established species and evolution line.

### CC-003 — Zerolynx ⚡
- **Concept:** Electric lynx kitten with sparking whiskers.
- **Battle identity:** Fast turn control, paralysis and critical-hit pressure.
- **Suggested starting moves:** Spark Claw, Quick Strike, Static Pulse, Focus Charge.
- **Field skill — Sparklink:** Powers designated switches and old machines.
- **Habitat:** Havenwood storm clearings and Stormreach outskirts.
- **Evolution:** Zerolynx → Voltraze (Lv 17) → Tempestrix (Lv 37).
- **Collection hook:** Opens ancient powered devices and favors speedy battle tactics.
- **Status:** Established species and evolution line.

### CC-004 — Noctyr 🌑
- **Concept:** Shadow wolf cub with violet eyes and a smoky tail.
- **Battle identity:** Evasion, enemy stat reduction and unpredictable attacks.
- **Suggested starting moves:** Shadow Swipe, Night Veil, Dread Howl, Phantom Bite.
- **Field skill — Veilsense:** Reveals marked hidden passages and Hollowing clues.
- **Habitat:** Rare Havenwood nighttime or shadow-zone encounter; later The Hollow.
- **Evolution:** Noctyr → Dreadveil (Lv 20) → Eclipsion (special story condition).
- **Collection hook:** Rare mystery creature connected to the central plot.
- **Status:** Established species; final evolution condition remains to be designed.

### CC-005 — Embernewt 🔥
- **Concept:** Fire salamander with glowing frills.
- **Battle identity:** Gradual damage through burn; less burst damage than Cindra.
- **Signature move concept:** Searing Trail — low damage with a chance to burn.
- **Field skill — Kindlespark:** Starts designated campfires and small braziers.
- **Habitat:** Ashfall Ridge thermal pools.
- **Evolution:** TBD; consider one evolved form that emphasizes sustained damage.
- **Collection hook:** Alternative Ignis playstyle for defensive teams.
- **Status:** Proposed, not yet implemented.

### CC-006 — Mossmoth 🌿

- **Battle identity:** Healing, status recovery and light disruption.
- **Signature move concept:** Pollen Veil — briefly reduces incoming special damage.
- **Field skill — Herbglow:** Highlights gatherable medicinal plants.
- **Habitat:** Havenwood flower groves.
- **Evolution:** TBD; consider a bond-based evolution.
- **Collection hook:** Makes exploration and resource discovery more rewarding.
- **Status:** Proposed, not yet implemented.

### CC-007 — Sparkrill ⚡

- **Habitat:** Stormreach outskirts and electric grassland.

- **Collection hook:** Suits investigation and resource-focused exploration.
- **Status:** Proposed, not yet implemented.

## Implementation data model (proposed)

Each **species** should define:
- `species_id`, `name`, `element`, `habitats`, `rarity`
- `base_stats`: hp, attack, defense, special_attack, special_defense, speed
- `learnset`: move IDs and learning levels
- `capture_base_rate`, `field_skill_id`, `evolutions`
- `sprite_key`, `description`, `encounter_weight`

Each **owned creature instance** should define:
- `instance_id`, `species_id`, `level`, `experience`
- `current_hp`, `moves` (maximum four), `status`
- `bond` (0–100), `nickname`, `evolution_postponed`

**Important:** Never store creature-specific current HP, bond or nickname in the shared species definition. Those belong to the individual captured creature.

## Development priorities

1. **Lock the first four species** and their moves, battle stats and field skill behavior.
2. **Implement active follower and one Havenwood field obstacle** (Thornback's Rootcall).
3. **Test capture → switch lead → follow → field skill → return to Rowan** on a phone.
4. **Add Mossmoth and Fernling** as the first new Havenwood encounters.
5. Expand to Ashfall Ridge, Stormreach and Blackveil species after the foundation works.

## Open design decisions
- Base stat values and growth curves for all 12 species.
- Exact move accuracy, status duration and cooldown/PP rules.
- Final names and requirements for the eight proposed evolution lines.
- Which species receive branching evolutions.
- Encounter rates, rarity tiers and capture tuning.
- Creature sprite art direction and animation budget.
